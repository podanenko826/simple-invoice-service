## 🎨 Social Media Images Specs

### Open Graph Image (og-image.png)
- Size: 1200x630px
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Max file size: <1MB
- Content: Logo + Tagline + Key Benefit
- Text should be readable at small sizes

### Twitter Card Image (twitter-image.png)
- Size: 1200x600px (2:1 ratio)
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Max file size: <1MB
- Similar content to OG image

### Design Tips
- Use brand colors
- Keep text minimal and large
- High contrast for readability
- Test on mobile devices
